Low blood sugar: what counts as low, and what to do

By Vishnu Purushothaman, who makes Companion · Checked against the sources below on October 19, 2026

If someone with diabetes can't swallow or is unconscious, it is an emergency: don't give food or drink. Use glucagon if there is some, turn them onto their side, and call your local emergency number.

A reading below 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L) is low, and below 54 mg/dL (3.0 mmol/L) is seriously low. The widely taught approach is 15 to 20 grams of fast-acting carbohydrate, a recheck after 15 minutes, and the same again if it's still low. Follow the plan your care team gave you.

The signs

Shakiness, sweating, hunger, a fast heartbeat, irritability, and confusion. A severe low can cause a seizure or passing out. Some people feel lows less clearly over the years; a meter or sensor reading settles it.

The 15-15 approach

  1. Take 15 to 20 g of fast-acting carbohydrate: glucose tablets (check the label for how many make 15 g), half a cup (120 ml) of juice or regular soda, or a tablespoon of sugar or honey.
  2. Wait 15 minutes and check again.
  3. Still low? Repeat.
  4. Once you're back in range, have a snack if your next meal is more than an hour or so away.

Chocolate and other fatty foods raise glucose more slowly, so they're a poor first choice for a low.

Lows at night, and sensors

A sensor that is pressed on, usually by lying on it in bed, can read falsely low: the pressure squeezes fluid away from it. It often shows as a sudden dip in the small hours that recovers as soon as you roll over. If a low appears and you feel fine, check with a fingerstick before treating. If you do feel low, treat it first and check afterwards.

Glucagon

Glucagon is the rescue medicine for a severe low, given by someone else as a nasal spray or a ready-to-use pen. If you take insulin or a sulfonylurea, it's worth having one at home and at work, checking its expiry date, and showing the people around you where it is. Setting up Medical ID in the Health app lets a paramedic see that you have diabetes from your Lock Screen.
