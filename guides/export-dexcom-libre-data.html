How to export your Dexcom or Libre glucose history

By Vishnu Purushothaman, who makes Companion · Checked against the sources below on October 19, 2026

Both services let you download your readings as a CSV file from their websites: in Dexcom Clarity use Export and choose the dates; in LibreView open Glucose History and choose Download glucose data. The file opens in any spreadsheet and can be imported into other apps.

Dexcom Clarity

  1. Sign in at clarity.dexcom.com with your Dexcom account.
  2. Choose Export (the arrow icon near the top of the page).
  3. Pick the date range and save the CSV file.

LibreView (FreeStyle Libre)

  1. Sign in at libreview.com with the account your Libre app or reader uses.
  2. Open Glucose History.
  3. Choose Download glucose data, confirm, and save the CSV file.

Both sites change their menus from time to time; if a button has moved, their help pages describe the current steps.

What's in the file

One row per reading: the time, the value in mg/dL or mmol/L, and for some rows the kind of reading (sensor, scan or fingerstick) and any events you logged. It's yours: open it in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, or send it to your care team.

Why export at all?

  • A long history in one place, independent of an account or a subscription.
  • Apple Health is late. Dexcom's app writes readings to Apple Health about three hours after they happen, and some Libre apps don't write to it at all, so apps that read from Health may be missing your recent sensor data. A CSV fills the gap.
  • Moving apps without losing the past.

Companion, the journal

Companion imports Dexcom Clarity and LibreView files directly (Profile → Import Dexcom or Libre export), in mg/dL or mmol/L, skipping any reading it already has from Apple Health. The readings stay on your iPhone, and appear on your day's line beside your meals.

No account, and nothing you log leaves your iPhone.
