Privacy Policy

Companion: Diabetes Journal · Effective September 8, 2026

Companion is built so that your health information stays with you. There is no Companion account, no Companion server, and nothing you log is sent to us. This page says exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device, and why.

What the app stores

Meals, glucose readings, activity, medications and doses, questions for your doctor, and your preferences are stored on your device in the app's own database. If you turn on Sync with iCloud, that data is also stored in your private iCloud database, encrypted in transit and at rest and readable only by your Apple Account. We cannot read it. iCloud sync is off by default.

What leaves your device

  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a product barcode, the barcode number alone is sent to Open Food Facts to fetch nutrition facts. No personal or health data is sent with it.
  • Photo meal recognition (optional, off by default). If you enable it, a reduced-size copy of the meal photo you choose is sent to the recognition service so it can list the foods. Nothing else is sent: no glucose, medication, notes, or name. You review every result before anything is logged.
  • Nothing else. Label scanning, voice logging, insights, the doctor report, and Ask My Data all run on the device. Apple Intelligence answers, where available, are generated on the device.

Apple Health

With your permission, Companion reads the Health categories you choose and can write glucose readings and the meals you log back to Health. Health data is used only to draw your timeline and summaries. It is never sent anywhere. You can change access at any time in the Health app under Sharing.

Analytics

Companion records that features were used, for example "a meal was logged". It never records what you logged. In the current version these events stay on the device and are not transmitted.

Purchases

Companion Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment and shares no payment details with us. Your subscription status is checked on your device.

Your control

Export everything as CSV from Profile. Delete all data from Profile removes everything Companion stored on the device and, if sync is on, in your private iCloud database. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Not medical advice

Companion organizes information you provide. It does not diagnose, prescribe, calculate doses, or replace advice from your healthcare team.
